Frequently Asked Questions

How does CopyCloud work?

Upload content on one device, receive a short retrieval code, then enter the code on another device to access the same content.

How long is data available?

Transfers remain available for 24 hours and are automatically deleted after expiration.

Is CopyCloud suitable for large backups?

No. CopyCloud is designed for temporary transfer and quick handoffs, not long-term storage.

What devices are supported?

Any modern browser on desktop or mobile can use CopyCloud for retrieval and upload.
