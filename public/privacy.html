Privacy Policy

Data handling

CopyCloud stores transfer data temporarily so you can retrieve it with a generated code. Content is not intended for permanent archiving.

Retention and deletion

  • Transfer data is automatically deleted after 24 hours.
  • Expired clips are removed from active storage.
  • CopyCloud is not designed as a backup system.

Anonymity and local data

  • No account creation is required for standard transfer usage.
  • Local history may be stored in your browser for convenience.
  • Users are responsible for keeping retrieval codes private.
