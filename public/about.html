About CopyCloud

What CopyCloud does

CopyCloud is a temporary online clipboard that helps you move text and files between devices using a short retrieval code. It is designed for moments when you need fast transfer without account setup or long-term storage.

Why it exists

Many workflows involve switching from phone to laptop or sharing with another person quickly. CopyCloud reduces that friction by combining simple upload and retrieval steps with automatic expiration.

Privacy-first approach

CopyCloud avoids unnecessary personal data collection and uses temporary storage with automatic deletion after 24 hours. This makes it suitable for short-lived transfers where convenience and discretion both matter.
